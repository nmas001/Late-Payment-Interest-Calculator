Late Payment Interest Calculator

How to calculate late payment interest

Late payment interest is calculated using the amount owed, the annual interest rate, and the number of days overdue. Use this calculator for simple interest or daily compounding to estimate interest and total due.

What interest rate should I use?

Use the rate in your contract when available. If you’re using a statutory rate, check the official rate that applies in your country or region.

Is this calculator legally binding?

No. This tool provides an estimate for convenience and is not legal advice.

Tip: For statutory interest, use your country’s standard rate or your contract rate.
